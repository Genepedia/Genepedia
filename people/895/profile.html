John Gotlieb Sinden

Doop getuie:Pieter Coen,Frederik Swiegers, Hester Cornelia Liebenberg, Alida Johanna Swiegers, Gotlieb Gerhardus Swiegers en Elsie Sophia van der Merwe.

Last Will and Testament of John and Elizabeth drawn up 22 September 1888 at Vaal Koppies, District of Hay nominate the longest lived to be his sole and universal heir- with responsibility for any minor children.
