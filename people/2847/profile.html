It is quite clear that Christiana did not marry Philip Frost as in both her Will and Death Notice she was still 'van Eetveld'. She was deserted by her husband and Philip had a child with Christiana during his marriage to Elizabeth Doughty. Christiana's baby, 'Chrissie' grew up under the care of Philip & Elizabeth Frost.

Will of Christiana Gertruida Cornelia van Eetveld ,born van Lelyveld.

I, Christiana Gertruida Cornelia van Eetveld of Uitenhague Town in her Majesty's Colony of the Cape of Good Hope, South Africa, being at this time not in good health of body but am certainly of sound disposing mind,

memory and understanding and believe myself perfectly at liberty to make a will that stand good in law and equity having been separated from my husband on November 17th 1837 and on July 19th 1838 he seized my household furniture of every description and left me without Bed or stool or pot to cook in.

I had no part of the money raised by the sale of my household goods and was left destitute of money and necessary articles to shift for my self and four minor children, neither have I any relief from him since November 7th 1837.

On September 4th 1838 he my husband left this town and I saw him no more. I wrote to him affectionately but received no answer. And now considering the great uncertainty of human existence I do make, constitute . appoint and ordain this my last will and Testament in

manner and form following.

And first I desire that whenever it shall please the Almighty in the wise and gracious dispensation of His providence to put a period to my earthly career that my moral remains be decently interred at the discretion of my executor herein after named and my debts of my own private property consisting of Household Furniture and cooking utensils. Titles for money I have none, the property left to me by my Grandmother in Holland to

amount of thousands of dollars I have never received one sliver of it either principle or interest .My husband by unjust means kept it all from me and now I have all my children fully under the power and control to govern and rule for me in my place and stead according to his discretion namely Mr Philip Frost, who I hereby appoint full guardian in to act for me in their behalf, who hereby appoint to be my sole executor here of investing him with full power of Assumption and Surrogation together with such other power as any other executor can possibly be entitled. I do solemnly declare this is my only and last Will and Testament and Witness whereof I have hereinto set my hand Seal this Fourth day of November in the year of our Lord Christ One Thousand Eight Hundred and Forty Two.

Signed, Sealed and delivered by the Testator as her last Will and Testarment in the (a blot covers this word) who in the presence of each other have herein to set our names as underwritten.

C. van Eetveld.

H.J. van Lelyveld (no2)

James FRost
