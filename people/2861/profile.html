Margaret Helena Keyter

Death Notice: KAB MOOC 6/9/330 1442 (1894)

This Death Notice refers to 4 sons from her marriage with Henry John Foster Davis; makes reference to 2 other sons born from a "former marriage of ... Davis with a former predeceased spouse"; and contains a valuable reference to a "grandson Ernest Seaton Smith born 12 January 1889 a predeceased daughter of ... Davis and Margaret Helena Davis".

However, it seems the author of the Death Notice had the facts about this grandson slightly confused:

Earnest Seaton Smith was born on 13 June 1888 (according to his mother's Death Notice); he was the son of Rosa Eleanor Smith (nee Davis), who was a daughter of Henry John Foster Davis and she was indeed "predeceased" by the time this Death Notice was prepared. (She died on 18 Aug 1888.) However, she was not a daughter of Henry John Foster and his second wife, Margaret Helena Keyter: she was in fact a daughter from his first marriage to Louisa Catherine Roach.

- Nico Conradie (15 Jan 2017)
