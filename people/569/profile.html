Verhoor as Kaapse Rebel.

Dit blyk dat hy op 5 Julie 1902 in Calvinia verhoor.

Hy het skuldig gepleit op 'n klagte van Hoogverraad dat hul wel Kaapse Rebelle was en die wapen teen die Britse Regering opgeneem het. Die rebelle is almal gevonnis nadat hulle skuldig gepleit het.

Hy is saam met sy broer Johannes Meintjes de Beer verhoor.

No. of accussed: 654 Name of accused: De Beer, Sybrand Abraham, Besjesfontein Die volgende oor "Proclamation 100 of 1902" aangehaal uit dieselfde bron: In May 1902 the Boer republics signed the Treaty of Vereeniging and ceased to be sovereign states.

The rebels were not eligible to participate in the Peace negotiations as they were not legitimate belligerents. According to an agreement between the Boer leaders and the Colonial Office, if a rebel laid down arms and pleaded guilty to High Treason under Proclamation 100 of 1902, he received a partial amnesty, was disfranchised and released.

Rebel officers were to be charged in court, and fines and prison sentences would be handed down.

The surrender of 3,442 rebels was accepted under Proclamation 100 of 1902.

An estimated 700 to 800 rebels under arms failed to lay down arms and left the Cape Colony and lived in the old Boer Republics where they were *de facto* immune from prosecution."
