Rosemary Lurie

1979:

  • Pregnant with boyfriend's daughter.

1980:

  • Boyfriend ("Bassie Coetzee") dies in car accident.
  • Had boyfriend's daughter (Moshelly Angeline Ashely Lurie).

+-1981-1989:

  • Lived and worked on a farm in Zimbabwe.

1989:

  • Moved to Petersburg in South Africa with her daughter (Moshelly Angeline Ashely Lurie).
  • Got a job at Salati Enterprises as a General Worked.

1989-1990:

  • Worked in Petersburg for a year to save money to immigrate to Australia or New Zealand.

1990:

  • Bought flight tickets for immigration.
  • Went to Bloemfontein to meet her sister (Delene Zenobia Lurie) 2 weeks before immigration.
  • Met a Pastor (Petrus Johannes Van Der Merwe) who tore up their flight tickets and asked them to stay in South Africa.

1991:

  • Pregant with Pastor's son.
  • Engaged to Pastor.
  • Created a church from their home called "The Hand of God Care Centre" in Grasslands, Bloemfontein.

1991:

  • Married Pastor (Petrus Johannes Van Der Merwe).
  • Had Pastor's son (Samuel Joshua Van Der Merwe).

1994:

  • Moved church to their new home in Noordhoek, Bloemfontein.

2006:

  • Pastor dies.
