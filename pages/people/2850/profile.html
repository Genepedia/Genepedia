Petrus Johannes Francois Henry van Lelyveld

Twin of Henrietta Jacoba Maria Magdalena Clignet van Lelyveld He was born 3 days premature to his twin sister, suffered brain damage and was mentally deficient

He died at his brother, Hendrik Jacob Clignet's home in Port Elizabeth
