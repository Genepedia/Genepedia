= Note =

The above Death Notice is problematic. His Age at Death appears to be "48 jaren", which would be completely inaccurate. His Date of Death is given as 28 July 1879, whilst his Estate (See Reference Above) gives his Date of Death as 20 July 1882. The names of his children appear to reconcile somewhat with the Baptism Records already located and his parents seems to be correct, except for the "Gert Jacobus" as his father's name. It is obvious that this document was drawn up quite some time after his death at the time when M J Barnard was seeking permission to remarry. Could the years that had elapsed since his death in either 1879 or 1882 until the completion of the Death Notice in 1885 have led to so many inaccuracies?
