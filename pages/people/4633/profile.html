Catharina Johanna Herbst

b3 Marthinus Johannes Hendrik = 19.3.1790 † Kapokfontein, Smithfield 6.2.1856 x Graaff-Reinet 26.12.1809 Rachel Charlotte BLOM

c7 Catharina Johanna * 21.2.1824 = Graaff-Reinet 14.3.1824 x Colesberg 16.5.1841 Gerhardus Albertus VAN ASWEGEN

"Inventory of the intestate estate of the late Catrina (SIC) Johanna born HERBST and her surviving husband Gerhardus Albertus van ASWEGEN was validated by Field Cornet Gideon Joubert at Colesburg on 28 Sep 1842 and filed 15 Oct 1842. Inventory: 1 x Wagon (old) = £9; 4 x Oxen = £4.16 and 19 x head of cattle = £14.5. Signed off by her husband on 24 Sep 1842. (Re: CT archives MOOC 7/1/169 ref 133)"
