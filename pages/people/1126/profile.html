Marriage

Baptism 28 May 1871 Potchefstroom: Jacoba Adriana Graaf and also Louiza Maria Snyman, cousins. Jacoba Adriana named after her mother Jacoba Adriana Snyman and her mother's sister in law, Jacoba Adriana Viljoen.

Witnesses for Jacoba Adriana: Jan Hendrik Snyman (Mother's brother), Jacoba Adriana Viljoen (Jan Hendrik Snyman's wife), Piet Ninanber and Martha Magdalena Snyman. https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-89Z5-R9RR?i=921&cc=2155416
