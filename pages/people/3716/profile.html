Gerrit Matthys Nel

GISA's SAG CD Vol. 6 provides duplicated and erroneous information pertaining to Gerrit Matthys Nel (b8c2d3e4) and Gerrit Matthys Nel (b8c1d3e2) (this profile).

The marriage with Christina Alida Strauss (as per SAG) is highly problematic, in my opinion. His Death Notice makes no reference to such a marriage. Also, in the time-line of his life events such a marriage cannot seem to "fit in".
