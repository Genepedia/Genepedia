Some info re Beaufort ( West ) : Beaufort West was the first town to be established in the central Karoo. The town was founded in =1818= and initially named Beaufort after Henry Somerset, 5th Duke of Beaufort, who was the father of Lord Charles Henry Somerset, then governor of the Cape Colony.[4] The town was renamed

= Beaufort West in 1869= to avoid confusion with Port Beaufort in the Western Cape as well as Fort Beaufort in the Eastern Cape.
