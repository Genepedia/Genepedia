Pieter Johannes van Niekerk

TAB MHG O/626

Also known in the Bloemhof area as Pieter "Voortrekker" van Niekerk.

He was probably also the AJ Van Niekerk who took part in the battle of Bloedrivier.

Initially he owned the farm Klipfontein, which he later swopped for the farm Kalkfontein of Stoffel Kotze. This way he had two adjacent farms, Rietfontein and Kalkfontein. The later owner of his farm Klipfontein sold it to William Barkley. The town Bloemhof was later established on Klipfontein. (Source: ID Van Niekerk (28/2/1876 - 18/2/1953), grandson of Pieter "Voortrekker" Johannes van Niekerk, and son of Izak David van Niekerk (1844-1895), as published in Famnea, May 2012).
